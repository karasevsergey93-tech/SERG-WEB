03 / Working together

How we work together

From our first conversation to handover, with an agreed scope and clear next steps.

01

Understand your business

We discuss your offer, customers and the task your website needs to support.

02

Agree on the scope

We define pages, features, price, payment terms and timeline, including the number of revision rounds.

03

Gather the materials

We prepare copy, photos, product or service details and contacts. We agree on what you supply and where you need my help.

04

Design and build

We approve the structure and visual direction before development, then check the website on phones and computers.

05

Launch and hand over

We check links, contact details and agreed features, publish the site, hand over access and explain how to use it.

06

Support after launch

Before launch, we agree on who updates the content, what support includes and which work is charged separately.
